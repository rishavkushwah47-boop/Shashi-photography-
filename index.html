About Shashi Photography Studio

Your moments.
Our vision.

Welcome to Shashi Photography Studio, a photography studio based in Jaitpur Kalan, Bah, Agra, Uttar Pradesh.

With around 5 years of experience, we focus on capturing genuine emotions and meaningful moments in a natural, elegant and cinematic style.

From wedding and pre-wedding celebrations to candid photography, cinematic photographs, cinematic videos, reels and high-quality albums, our aim is to create photographs and films that you will love for years to come.

For us, good photography is only one part of the experience. Comfortable communication, respectful behaviour and understanding what the customer actually wants are equally important.
