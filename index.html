About Us

5+ years of experience in capturing moments that matter

Welcome to Shashi Photography Studio, a professional photography studio based in Jaitpur Kalan, Bah, Agra, Uttar Pradesh.

With more than 5 years of experience, we specialise in capturing genuine emotions, beautiful details and unforgettable moments through creative and cinematic photography.

From weddings and candid moments to portraits, cinematic films, reels and drone photography, every project is created with attention to quality and according to what the customer wants.

For us, great photography is not only about the final picture. We believe that good behaviour, comfortable communication and customer satisfaction are equally important.
